Why evaporative

A cooler way to cool.

Air drawn through a saturated honeycomb pad gives up its heat to evaporating water — leaving the air 5–12°C cooler. No compressor, no refrigerant, no sealed room.

Up to 12°C cooler

Evaporation pulls heat directly from the air stream — drops temperature without chilling the room.

80% less energy

A 1,500 CMH cooler runs on ~65 W. The largest 18,000 CMH unit still uses a fraction of split A/C power.

Fresh air, always

Designed for open spaces. Doors and windows stay open — air keeps moving, never recirculating.

Free HK delivery

Same-day delivery across Hong Kong, Kowloon, and the New Territories. 1-year warranty on every unit.
