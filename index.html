/01

Cooling that breathes.

Hong Kong runs hot. Air-conditioning answers with sealed rooms, dripping condensers, and a 1.5 kW compressor that won't quit. Evaporative cooling answers differently — pull warm air across a saturated honeycomb pad, the water phase-changes, and the air leaves 5 to 12°C cooler. No refrigerant. No sealed envelope. Doors open. Fresh air always moving.

/01

Hot air in

Ambient air drawn in through honeycomb cooling pads on three sides of the unit.

/02

Water absorbs heat

A circulating pump keeps the pads saturated. Water evaporates, pulling heat out of the air stream.

/03

Cool air out

Centrifugal blower delivers up to 38,000 m³/h of cool, humidified, filtered air at low static pressure.

/04

Reset

10-200 L tanks. Mains-fill option. Anti-bacterial pads. 80–90% less energy than refrigerant A/C.
